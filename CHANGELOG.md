### Added

- `(threading)`: `~>`, `~>>`, `and~>`, `and~>>`, the `lambda` threading
  forms and their `λ` aliases, with R6RS `_` insertion holes.
- `(threading)`: `when~>`, `unless~>`, `cond~>` and `tee~>`.
- Reference documentation and syntax errors that name the original form and
  invalid clause.
